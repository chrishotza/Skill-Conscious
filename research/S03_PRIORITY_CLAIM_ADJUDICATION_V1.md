# S03 — Priority Claim Adjudication V1

## Scope

This document adjudicates the 31 priority candidates exported by S03.8.

The adjudication is **exploratory and descriptive**. These labels are researcher
judgments from the claim text and are not source-provided ground truth.

## Classes

- **P1** — direct/substantive phenomenological structure: organization of
  experience, perception, self-awareness, attention, embodiment, first-person
  experience, intersubjective experience, or experienced state transitions.
- **P2** — theoretical phenomenology: a model or theory about phenomenological
  organization rather than a direct experiential description.
- **M1** — meta/epistemic/methodological commentary.
- **O1** — ontological/metaphysical claim.
- **H1** — historical/bibliographic claim.
- **N1** — normative/comparative methodology.

## Preliminary result

Strict P1 direct phenomenological claims: **9 / 31**.

P1 families represented:

- Filosofía/fenomenología
- Ciencia/IA
- Contemplativo/transpersonal
- Budismo
- Tántrico/śaiva
- Indígena/relacional

Thus the 31-claim priority queue contains a **six-family P1 candidate set**.

This is **not** yet evidence of independent cross-cultural convergence because:
1. the candidate queue was seeded from a metadata-defined set concentrated in four
   families;
2. several P1 claims are semantically linked to those seeds;
3. there is no source-independence test here;
4. adjudication is exploratory and researcher-coded.

## Candidate phenomenological dimensions

The P1 subset suggests the following dimensions for further testing:

1. **Prereflexive self-awareness**
2. **Embodied experience**
3. **Attention / access**
4. **Altered experiential states**
5. **De-identification from conceptual thought**
6. **Intersubjective experience**
7. **Experienced expansion of relational structure**
8. **Situated bodily selfhood**

These are **candidate dimensions**, not an established theory.

## Key methodological consequence

The corpus now contains enough P1-like material to justify a dedicated
cross-family phenomenological analysis, but the next test must be designed
around **source-family independence and source lineage**, not merely semantic
similarity.

The strongest candidates for a future replication are those dimensions that
have:
- multiple families;
- multiple independent corpus units;
- non-overlapping source lineages;
- explicit experiential content;
- and semantic convergence after removing the seed-source dependence.

## Guardrail

No claim in this document establishes fundamental consciousness,
panpsychism, metaphysical truth, or causal mechanism.
