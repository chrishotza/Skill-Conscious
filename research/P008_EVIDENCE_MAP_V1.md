## Competing explanations

P008 must discriminate at minimum among:

1. **Causal self-reference** — the self-model itself changes future trajectory.
2. **Memory-only explanation** — the apparent effect is fully explained by extra stored information.
3. **Capacity/context explanation** — the effect comes from additional computation or context length.
4. **Generic control explanation** — any persistent internal variable would produce the same effect.
5. **Prompting explanation** — the observed change is induced by external textual instructions rather than endogenous state.

## Operational construct

`SelfModelCausalInfluence`:

> The change in future trajectory-selection attributable specifically to an intervention on the self-model, after controlling the candidate-future set, external input, randomization, compute budget, and stored information.

## Primary prediction

A targeted self-model intervention should produce a reproducible trajectory-selection divergence relative to a matched control where the self-model is present but causally disconnected.

## Null

`H0:` once memory, compute, inputs and candidate futures are matched, self-model intervention produces no specific change in trajectory selection.

## Failure criteria

The hypothesis is weakened when:

- intervention produces no specific effect;
- the effect disappears under memory-matched control;
- a generic internal-state variable produces the same effect;
- prompt/context changes explain the effect;
- effects fail replication across deterministic seeds.
