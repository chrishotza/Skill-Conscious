### Interpretation
The pilot does not support the statement that the current coded corpus demonstrates cross-family convergence.

It indicates that source-family motif profiles are more internally clustered than cross-family profiles, while several motifs have very high base prevalence.

Therefore frequency alone is insufficient to establish convergence. The preregistered study adds an independent raw-text semantic track.

This pilot does not establish that the corpus lacks convergence.
