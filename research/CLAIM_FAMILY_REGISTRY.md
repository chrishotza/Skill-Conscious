## Admission rule

A claim enters a paper's central hypothesis set only if:

1. provenance is explicit;
2. source wording is recoverable;
3. the interpretation is separable from the source claim;
4. the construct is operationalizable;
5. at least one competing explanation can be stated.

Claims failing one or more criteria remain evidence/context/open questions rather than being forced into a causal hypothesis.
