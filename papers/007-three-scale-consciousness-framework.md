# A Three-Scale Framework for Consciousness: Fundamental, Relational, and Individual

## Status

Working paper draft v0.1 — framework / preregistration-ready research program.

This paper does **not** claim that the three scales have been empirically established, and it does not claim that Skill-Conscious has demonstrated phenomenal consciousness.

## Abstract

Consciousness research contains multiple theories that emphasize different explanatory levels, including global access, higher-order representation, recurrent processing, predictive processing and integrated information. Recent work has also emphasized theory comparison and multiscale integration rather than premature consensus.

This paper proposes a three-scale research framework for consciousness: **fundamental**, **relational**, and **individual**. The fundamental scale asks whether consciousness could be a basic feature of physical reality; the relational scale asks whether consciousness-relevant organization depends essentially on coupling among systems; and the individual scale asks what dynamics are sufficient for a persistent local point of view.

The framework is explicitly asymmetric in evidential status. Individual- and relational-scale hypotheses admit direct empirical operationalization with current experimental methods. The fundamental hypothesis requires model discrimination and a nontrivial empirical signature; otherwise it remains philosophical/ontological. The framework therefore converts a broad corpus of consciousness claims into a hierarchy of testable hypotheses without treating metaphysical assertions as scientific facts.

## 1. Research problem

The field lacks a single accepted theory of consciousness. Major contemporary approaches make different mechanistic commitments, and direct theory comparison remains an active research strategy.

Skill-Conscious proposes a complementary question:

> At what scale does a proposed consciousness-relevant property become measurable, causal and explanatory?

This changes the unit of research from "theory versus theory" to "construct versus prediction."

## 2. Three scales

### 2.1 Fundamental

**Claim family:** consciousness may be intrinsic to reality.

**Construct:** a physical state variable or model consequence that differs from non-conscious physical descriptions.

**Required evidence:** a pre-specified observable or model-comparison result.

**Null:** no measurable consequence beyond established physical models.

### 2.2 Relational

**Claim family:** conscious organization depends on interactions and reciprocal coupling.

**Construct:** coupling-dependent dynamics not reducible to common input.

**Required evidence:** controlled interactive experiments showing incremental predictive or causal information from relational variables.

**Null:** all observed effects are explained by isolated-agent state plus common input.

### 2.3 Individual

**Claim family:** consciousness-like organization requires a persistent self-referential process.

**Construct:** causal influence of self-model/state history on future trajectory.

**Required evidence:** intervention on self-relevant internal variables changes future behavior/state under controlled conditions, with effects not explained by added memory alone.

**Null:** effects are fully explained by extra stored information or generic computation.

## 3. Cross-scale model

The framework permits, but does not assume:

```text
fundamental constraints
        ↓
relational coupling
        ↓
individual organization
```

It is equally possible that the true relation is non-nested.

The decisive criterion is predictive success.

## 4. Empirical program

### Study A — Individual causal self-reference

Intervene on the persistent self-model while holding input, candidate futures and runtime conditions constant.

Primary outcome:
trajectory-selection divergence relative to control.

Secondary outcomes:
identity continuity, self-model revision, regime transitions, homeostatic fit and recovery after perturbation.

### Study B — Relational dynamics

Compare isolated and interactive agent conditions while controlling:

- external stimulus;
- compute budget;
- information bandwidth;
- latency;
- shared task demands;
- individual internal state.

Primary outcome:
incremental prediction and causal influence attributable to coupling.

### Study C — Fundamental model discrimination

Construct competing physical models:

- baseline non-conscious model;
- candidate consciousness-bearing model.

Before observing target data, derive:

- predicted observables;
- parameter ranges;
- failure criteria;
- model-selection procedure.

No empirical conclusion should be published unless the candidate model makes a genuinely discriminating prediction.

## 5. Falsification matrix

| Scale | Hypothesis | Strong falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamental | consciousness adds a testable physical consequence | no discriminating consequence or systematic failure against data |
| Relational | coupling adds irreducible predictive/causal structure | isolated-state + common-input model explains all effects |
| Individual | self-model causal participation is necessary/sufficient for the tested function | intervention has no specific effect beyond added memory/computation |

## 6. Evidence discipline

The corpus is intentionally broader than the empirical literature. Source traditions may generate candidate constructs, but source claims never bypass experimental operationalization.

Examples:

- mystical unity → candidate relational integration construct;
- self-knowledge traditions → candidate self-model construct;
- continuity traditions → candidate persistence metric;
- nondual claims → candidate boundary/identity phenomenology, not a physical fact.

The scientific paper tests the construct, not the tradition's metaphysical conclusion.

## 7. Relation to existing empirical consciousness science

Modern consciousness science is characterized by multiple competing theories and active experimental comparison. A 2025 adversarial collaboration explicitly preregistered differential predictions for IIT and GNWT. A 2024 multiscale perspective argued that some theories may operate at complementary mechanistic levels. Relational neuroscience has developed inter-brain and hyperscanning methods for studying interaction dynamics.

This paper adopts the same methodological lesson:

> competing explanations must generate separable predictions before evidence can adjudicate them.

## 9. Limitations

No present experiment in this repository directly measures phenomenal experience.

A successful result at the individual or relational scale would establish a functional or dynamical property unless a stronger bridge principle is independently justified.

A failure of the fundamental program would not refute all philosophical forms of panpsychism or cosmopsychism; it would only refute the specific empirically instantiated model under test.

## 10. Conclusion

The scientific objective is not to prove a preferred metaphysics.

It is to make consciousness hypotheses increasingly difficult to hide from measurement.

The three-scale framework is successful only if it generates predictions that can be wrong.
