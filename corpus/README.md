# Consciousness Corpus — Corpus v1

## Purpose

This corpus is the historical and comparative foundation of Skill-Conscious.

The project does not begin by assuming that consciousness is exhausted by a modern scientific definition. It first asks what human traditions have claimed consciousness, awareness, selfhood, mind, soul, spirit, interiority, presence, being, or ultimate reality to be.

The working principle is:

> preserve the source claim first; normalize it second; search for recurrence across independent traditions third; derive the convergent metaphysical model only after the corpus is assembled.

## Corpus architecture

SOURCE → PRIMARY CLAIM → PHENOMENOLOGICAL CLAIM → ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM → METAPHYSICAL MOTIF → CROSS-CULTURAL RECURRENCE → CONVERGENT INVARIANT → PROJECT INTERPRETATION → ENGINEERING MAPPING → EMPIRICAL PREDICTION

## What counts as a source

The corpus includes primary scriptures and philosophical works; historical esoteric and occult texts; contemplative manuals and first-person traditions; anthropological and comparative source collections; modern phenomenological and philosophical works; scientific consciousness literature; heterodox/extended-consciousness literature; and project-original sources.

Primary sources receive priority for claims about what a tradition actually taught.

## Anti-collapse rule

Do not silently convert Atman into "self-model", qi into "energy variable", Nous into "global workspace", Ein Sof into "field", Tao into "information", gnosis into "metacognition", spirit into "subsystem", or mystical unity into "integration".

Those may become engineering analogies later, but the original claim remains distinct.

## Convergence rule

A motif enters the Convergent Core when it recurs across independent traditions or source families after normalization.

The project is not choosing one tradition as winner.

The research question is:

> What remains when the vocabulary is stripped away but the recurring ontological structure is preserved?

## Source-card minimum

Every source card should eventually contain source_id, tradition, cultural region, date/period, author or school, primary/secondary status, canonical title, transmission history, exact claim, phenomenological description, ontological interpretation, metaphysical interpretation, self model, world model, body model, time model, transformation model, relation to death, relation to other minds, method/practice, textual citation, modern scholarship, implementation hypothesis, and uncertainty/provenance notes.

## Current scope

Corpus v1 begins with a broad register of ancient, medieval, early-modern, modern, indigenous/comparative, esoteric, and scientific sources. The target is 200+ sources, not because source count proves a proposition, but because the synthesis should not depend on a narrow canon.
