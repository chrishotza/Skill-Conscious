## Comparative note

This batch links three very different patterns: positive Buddha-nature language, empty-but-knowing Mahāmudrā awareness, and highly structured emanation/repair in Kabbalah, alongside Rumi's relational Sufi transformation. The overlap is structural, not a claim of metaphysical identity.
