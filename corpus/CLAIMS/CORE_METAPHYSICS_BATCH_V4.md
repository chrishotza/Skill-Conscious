## Divergence and caution log

- Bhagavad Gita/Phaedo and Egyptian funerary material support strong continuity/persistence claims, while Buddhist source families elsewhere in the corpus reject a permanent self; persistence and self-substance therefore must remain separate variables.
- Dao De Jing and Zhuangzi often dissolve fixed subject/object categories rather than positing a permanent metaphysical ego.
- Gospel of Thomas uses compact aphoristic and symbolic language; motifs such as 'kingdom within/without' must not be equated with a literal field or neural substrate.
- The Cloud of Unknowing treats darkness as a mode of unknowing, not a sensory state; this strengthens M14 while warning against operationally equating ineffability with low information.
