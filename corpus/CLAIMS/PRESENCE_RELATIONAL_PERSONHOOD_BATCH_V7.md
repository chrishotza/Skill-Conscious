## Comparative value

- Suhrawardi contributes a particularly precise bridge between **self-awareness, presence, ontology, and epistemology**: awareness is not merely a report about a system; in his model, self-presence helps define how knowing occurs. The Stanford Encyclopedia notes that his account gives self-awareness logical priority to ordinary subject/object division and develops knowledge-by-presence from that basis. citeturn409200search5
- Yoruba Orí, Māori whakapapa, Haudenosaunee gratitude, and Tempels's Bantu force ontology all strengthen M16 relational/distributed personhood, but they do so through very different cultural models. They must never be collapsed into one generic 'indigenous consciousness' category. The Yoruba material is explicitly a modern scholarly synthesis; Māori whakapapa is presented as a lineage linking people, living things, earth, sky, and cosmological origins; the Haudenosaunee Address centers ritual gathering, reciprocal relations, and collective mind; Tempels is a missionary interpretation with well-known representational limitations. citeturn323229search3turn589021view3turn589021view1turn409200search1
