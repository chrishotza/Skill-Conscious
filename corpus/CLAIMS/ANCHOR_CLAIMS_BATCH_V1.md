## Extraction contract

- **P1** direct primary text; **P2** primary text through a traceable scholarly/curated edition or translation.
- Claims can map to several motifs; motif mapping never rewrites the source claim.
- Recurrence is not convergence until independent source families and semantic independence are verified.
- No historical claim is treated as proof that an artificial system is phenomenally conscious.
