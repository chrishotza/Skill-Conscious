## Comparative fault line

Madhyamaka refuses intrinsic essence; Yogacara models consciousness as a conditioned, multi-level process; Spanda and Vijnana Bhairava affirm an underlying conscious principle; Ibn Arabi articulates unity-with-difference. These are not averaged together. The synthesis must preserve the disagreement while asking whether a common structural layer exists beneath the incompatible ontologies.
