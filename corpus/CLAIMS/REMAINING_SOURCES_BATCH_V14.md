## Interpretation controls

- Haṭha Yoga sources are not treated as interchangeable; their bodily, subtle-physiological, and contemplative models remain source-specific.
- The Yoga Vāsiṣṭha's recensions/abridgements require edition-specific location mapping.
- Śaiva Pratyabhijñā claims are not collapsed into Advaita Vedānta or treated as universally shared metaphysics.
- Claims about liberation, subtle channels, or universal consciousness are textual/doctrinal claims, not independently established experimental results.
