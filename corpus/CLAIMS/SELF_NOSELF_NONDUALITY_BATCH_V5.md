## Major fault lines preserved

- **Self as ultimate identity:** Brihadaranyaka and Chandogya give strong identity/presence formulations.
- **Consciousness without a self-substance:** Bahiya and Heart Sutra constrain that interpretation by refusing fixed self or inherent essence.
- **Nonduality:** Vimalakirti dissolves paired oppositions without simply replacing them with another object.
- **Reflexive certainty:** Descartes grounds certainty in first-person thinking, but his substance dualism differs sharply from embodied/relational models.
- **Process and return:** Dao De Jing and Zhuangzi treat transformation, return, stillness, and standpoint as central without requiring a Cartesian subject.
