## Verification policy

- P2 = primary text through a traceable translation/edition.
- S1 = specialist/secondary evidence or school-level reconstruction; these claims cannot enter the highest-confidence convergent core without later primary-text verification.
- Motif labels are analytical tags, not translations of the source ontology.
- Divergent doctrines are retained as evidence, not normalized away.

## Divergence signals preserved

- Katha Upanishad: the text models a distinction between Self and the embodied cognitive system; this is an affirmative self-metaphysics.
- Anattalakkhana: consciousness itself is declared not-self; this is a direct counterexample to treating “self-presence” as synonymous with a permanent self-substance.
- Platform Sutra: no-thought is explicitly not simple thought-erasure; awareness and non-attachment are framed as compatible.
- Orphic material: post-mortem identity and Memory operate inside a ritual-cosmological framework rather than a purely psychological one.

These divergences must remain visible in the eventual Synthetic Metaphysical Ontology.
