**Status:** initial broad register. It is deliberately larger than the current verified source-card set and contains candidate primary, traditional, scholarly, scientific, and heterodox sources for staged verification.

**Target:** 200+ sources, 1,000+ extracted claims, and a traceable card for every retained source.

**Important:** inclusion in the register does not endorse a claim as true. Heterodox and occult works are retained because the project is studying the history of consciousness concepts, not because their metaphysics has been established empirically.

## Register policy

This register is intentionally broad. It is a **research index**, not yet a verified bibliography.

Before a source enters the Convergent Core, the project must verify:
- exact title and author/school;
- date and transmission history where relevant;
- primary or secondary status;
- canonical edition or authoritative digital text;
- exact consciousness-related passages;
- translation issues;
- whether the normalized motif preserves the original meaning.

The next stage converts the register into source cards and a claim-level matrix.
