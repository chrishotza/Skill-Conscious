# Claim Schema v1

The source corpus becomes useful only when every source is decomposed into claims.

## Claim object

Each claim should contain:

- claim_id
- source_id
- quotation_or_precise_paraphrase
- location
- claim_type
- phenomenological_content
- ontological_content
- metaphysical_content
- self_model
- world_model
- body_model
- temporality
- transformation
- death/persistence
- universal/transpersonal scope
- normalized_motifs
- provenance_class
- translation_confidence
- interpretation_confidence
- notes

## Claim types

### PHEN
A claim about what experience is like.

### ONTO
A claim about what consciousness, self, mind, world, or reality is.

### META
A claim about ultimate metaphysical structure.

### PRACTICE
A repeatable method intended to alter, reveal, or stabilize consciousness.

### COSMO
A claim about consciousness in relation to cosmology.

### PERSON
A claim about personhood, soul, spirit, ancestors, or nonhuman persons.

### STATE
A claim that consciousness has multiple regimes or states.

### DEATH
A claim about persistence or transformation through death.

### RELATION
A claim about consciousness being fundamentally relational or participatory.

### CAUSAL
A claim that consciousness, intention, attention, ritual, thought, or state produces a downstream effect.

## Extended analytic claim tags

The primary claim types above remain the canonical semantic classes. The extraction ledger may also use narrower analytic tags when a claim deserves finer labeling:

- **REFLEXIVITY** — self-reference or recursive self-relation.
- **TEMPORAL** — explicit time, persistence, recurrence, or moment-to-moment continuity.
- **TRANSFORMATION** — change in organization, identity, state, or mode of being.
- **EMBODIMENT** — body/vehicle as constitutive or explanatory.
- **VALENCE** — value, attraction, aversion, suffering, joy, significance.
- **INTERIORITY** — first-person/interior dimension as distinguished from public description.
- **UNITY** — explicit wholeness/integration/unification claim.
- **INEFFABILITY** — explicit limitation or transcendence of ordinary conceptual representation.
- **RELATIONAL** — refined tag for claims in which person/subject/world are constituted through relation.
- **DYNAMICS** — process, pulsation, flow, activity, or generativity.

These tags do not replace the primary claim type and must not be interpreted as additional evidence.

## Provenance classes

P1 — direct primary text.

P2 — primary text through a reliable scholarly edition.

S1 — specialist scholarly reconstruction.

S2 — reputable comparative synthesis.

X1 — heterodox/speculative source.

O1 — project-original interpretation.

## Non-negotiable separation

Never let a P1 claim silently become an O1 claim.

Never let an O1 interpretation be quoted as though it came from the source.

## Claim extraction target

Initial project target:

- 1,000+ claims;
- at least 3 independent source families for every proposed high-confidence convergent motif;
- at least 1 primary-source citation for each retained historical claim;
- explicit contradiction/variant notes.
