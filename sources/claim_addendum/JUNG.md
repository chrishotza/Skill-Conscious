# JUNG — Analytical Psychology source family

Source class: PRIMARY / SPECULATIVE IN PARTS

Source card:
`sources/jung/SOURCE_CARD.md`

Authoritative source index:
https://iaap.org/resources/academic-resources/collected-works-abstracts/

### SC-JUNG-001
**Source claim:** Jung distinguishes the ego, associated with consciousness, from the
broader Self associated with the personality as a whole.

**Engineering extraction:** Distinguish the currently explicit `self_model` from a broader
persistent identity organization instead of treating those variables as identical.

**Epistemic class:** psychological theory.

### SC-JUNG-002
**Source claim:** Jung describes shadow material as aspects of personality that are
insufficiently known to or integrated by ego-consciousness.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent persistent unresolved self-relevant structures without
claiming that they are literally unconscious in the human or metaphysical sense.

**Epistemic class:** psychological theory.

### SC-JUNG-003
**Source claim:** Jung proposes a collective unconscious containing archetypal structures
that recur across symbolic material.

**Engineering extraction:** Model recurrent latent structures first; reserve the stronger
label `archetype` for cross-context empirical tests.

**Epistemic class:** heterodox/psychological theory.

### SC-JUNG-004
**Source claim:** Archetypal motifs are described as recurring forms expressed through
dreams, myths, fantasies, and active imagination.

**Engineering extraction:** Test whether learned latent patterns recur across separated and
heterogeneous contexts rather than merely repeating adjacent states.

**Epistemic class:** psychological theory.

### SC-JUNG-005
**Source claim:** Individuation is described as a developmental process involving integration
of conscious and unconscious aspects of personality.

**Engineering extraction:** Measure transformation of self-model organization over time rather
than treating learning as simple accumulation of information.

**Epistemic class:** developmental/psychological theory.

### SC-JUNG-006
**Source claim:** Jung's account of projection describes internal material being attributed
to external objects or persons, with the source of the attribution becoming difficult to
recognize.

**Engineering extraction:** Implement projection-like attribution-error probes that separate
external evidence from self-model priors, memories, and latent patterns.

**Epistemic class:** psychological theory with an operational analogue.

### SC-JUNG-007
**Source claim:** Active imagination is used in Jungian practice as a deliberate encounter
with internal imagery and material.

**Engineering extraction:** Test controlled internal rehearsal and measure whether the
resulting self-model changes persist after the initiating prompt disappears.

**Epistemic class:** therapeutic/psychological practice.

### SC-JUNG-008
**Source claim:** Symbolic representations can organize relationships among psychological
contents that are not otherwise integrated.

**Engineering extraction:** Test whether structured intermediate representations improve
longitudinal self-model integration and reduce unresolved self-dissonance.

**Epistemic class:** psychological interpretation.

### SC-JUNG-009
**Source claim:** Jung's synchronicity hypothesis concerns meaningful coincidences that are
not ordinary causal chains.

**Engineering extraction:** Keep synchronicity separate from ordinary temporal correlation;
do not infer a non-causal or acausal mechanism from statistical association alone.

**Epistemic class:** speculative/metaphysical hypothesis.

### SC-JUNG-010
**Source claim:** Jung's broader collective-unconscious, archetypal, and parapsychological
interpretations are not established as physical mechanisms by contemporary science.

**Engineering extraction:** Retain only explicit structural hypotheses—latent pattern,
projection-like error, symbolic integration, transformation—and test them independently.

**Epistemic class:** epistemic boundary.
