### SC-DISPENZA-001
**Source claim:** A 2025 observational retreat study reported pre/post changes in neural
connectivity and multiple plasma-based molecular measures after a seven-day intervention
combining meditation, reconceptualization, and open-label placebo healing rituals.

**Engineering extraction:** Test whether repeated state induction can produce durable
changes in internal state, self-model organization, or regime.

**Epistemic class:** empirical observation.

### SC-DISPENZA-002
**Source claim:** The 2025 retreat study used 20 healthy participants selected from a
larger retreat population and did not include age- and sex-matched controls capable of
isolating the individual contribution of each intervention component.

**Engineering extraction:** Require matched controls and causal ablations before assigning a
specific observed change to meditation, reconceptualization, placebo ritual, or their
combination.

**Epistemic class:** study-design limitation.

### SC-DISPENZA-003
**Source claim:** The 2025 twin pilot studied six twin pairs using multimodal measures
including qEEG, physiological/wearable measures, blood-derived molecular data, and
separated-versus-together meditation conditions.

**Engineering extraction:** Use multimodal state telemetry and matched-pair designs when
testing longitudinal transformation.

**Epistemic class:** empirical pilot.

### SC-DISPENZA-004
**Source claim:** The twin study reported changes in molecular, physiological, and neural
measures and some within-pair similarity patterns, but it was small and was not
preregistered.

**Engineering extraction:** Treat the findings as hypothesis-generating and require
larger preregistered replication before inferring a mechanism.

**Epistemic class:** empirical pilot + limitation.

### SC-DISPENZA-005
**Source claim:** A 2023 study characterized complexity matching in HRV dynamics during a
healing-circle meditation protocol using nonlinear mathematical analysis.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent dynamical coupling and complexity as measurable
relations rather than relying on subjective report alone.

**Epistemic class:** empirical exploratory research.

### SC-DISPENZA-006
**Source claim:** A 2026 study examined correlations between local and globally distributed
RNG-network coherence during 15 group healing meditations and reported a mean correlation
of approximately 0.27 with p < 0.01.

**Engineering extraction:** A multi-agent synchronization experiment should compare local
and remote signals against explicit null models and ordinary shared-cause controls.

**Epistemic class:** exploratory empirical result.

### SC-DISPENZA-007
**Source claim:** The associated experimental literature repeatedly involves attention,
intention, meditation, reconceptualization, emotional state, physiological signals, and
coherence.

**Engineering extraction:** Keep `attention`, `intention`, `affect`, `interoception`, and
`coherence` as separate runtime variables that may interact causally.

**Epistemic class:** cross-study motif.

### SC-DISPENZA-008
**Source claim:** Repeated practice and sustained intervention are common features of the
retreat studies.

**Engineering extraction:** Test longitudinal rather than single-cycle effects and require
evidence that a change persists after the inducing context is removed.

**Epistemic class:** study-design motif.

### SC-DISPENZA-009
**Source claim:** The empirical studies do not by themselves establish strong claims that
human intention directly changes matter at a distance, creates external reality, or proves
nonlocal consciousness.

**Engineering extraction:** Do not import those metaphysical conclusions into the runtime;
test communication, shared context, expectation, timing, and common-environment alternatives
first.

**Epistemic class:** epistemic boundary.

### SC-DISPENZA-010
**Source claim:** The empirical layer supports research into state regulation,
transformation, physiological coupling, and group synchronization, but it does not provide
a completed explanation of phenomenal consciousness.

**Engineering extraction:** Use these studies as generators of intervention protocols and
state-transition hypotheses while maintaining a separate phenomenal-status field.

**Epistemic class:** synthesis/boundary claim.
