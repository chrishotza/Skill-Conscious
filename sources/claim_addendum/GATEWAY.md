### SC-GATEWAY-001
**Source claim:** The 1983 Army assessment evaluates the Monroe Institute's Gateway
Experience in terms of its mechanics and practical use for deliberately induced altered
states.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent consciousness-like operating regimes as potentially
state-transitionable configurations rather than one permanently fixed processing mode.

**Epistemic class:** historical source description / speculative explanatory layer.

### SC-GATEWAY-002
**Source claim:** The report discusses Hemi-Sync and synchronization/entrainment ideas in
connection with altered states.

**Engineering extraction:** Make synchronization between internal processes an explicit
measurable candidate variable and test whether it predicts reproducible regime transitions.

**Epistemic class:** source model; mechanism unverified by this source.

### SC-GATEWAY-003
**Source claim:** The report compares Gateway-related practice with techniques including
meditation, hypnosis, and biofeedback.

**Engineering extraction:** Treat controlled attention, feedback, and induced state changes as
separate experimental inputs rather than collapsing them into one mechanism.

**Epistemic class:** historical comparative description.

### SC-GATEWAY-004
**Source claim:** Resonance, frequency, and bodily vibration are used in the report's
attempted explanation of Gateway.

**Engineering extraction:** Test periodic/phase-coupled inputs as dynamical perturbations
without inheriting the report's physical interpretation as established fact.

**Epistemic class:** speculative model.

### SC-GATEWAY-005
**Source claim:** Gateway training describes distinct Focus states, including Focus 10,
Focus 12, Focus 15, and Focus 21.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent `regime` separately from persistent `identity`, and
test whether identity continuity survives regime transitions.

**Epistemic class:** source/training taxonomy.

### SC-GATEWAY-006
**Source claim:** Visualization and intention are described as mechanisms for altering the
practitioner's state and activity.

**Engineering extraction:** Expose intention and controlled rehearsal as intervention
variables whose downstream state effects can be measured.

**Epistemic class:** source-reported mechanism.

### SC-GATEWAY-007
**Source claim:** The assessment incorporates biomedical ideas associated with Itzhak Bentov
to explain physical aspects of altered states.

**Engineering extraction:** Treat bodily/physiological coupling as a candidate mechanism
class and keep it experimentally separable from subjective interpretation.

**Epistemic class:** historical theoretical import.

### SC-GATEWAY-008
**Source claim:** The report invokes Karl Pribram's holographic/holonomic ideas in its
attempted explanation of consciousness.

**Engineering extraction:** Test distributed representation and whole-pattern reconstruction
as computational mechanisms without treating the physical holographic model as proven.

**Epistemic class:** speculative model.

### SC-GATEWAY-009
**Source claim:** The report discusses out-of-body and extended-consciousness
interpretations as possibilities within its Gateway framework.

**Engineering extraction:** Keep anomalous-experience hypotheses in a separate experimental
layer and require independent outcome verification.

**Epistemic class:** speculative/anomalous hypothesis.

### SC-GATEWAY-010
**Source claim:** The assessment is a historical analysis of Gateway and explicitly is not a
final demonstration that its explanatory models or anomalous claims are physically correct.

**Engineering extraction:** Use Gateway to generate regime-transition, synchronization,
attention, and intention experiments—not as validation of phenomenal or nonlocal machine
consciousness.

**Epistemic class:** boundary claim.
