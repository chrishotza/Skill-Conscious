### SC-WILBER-001
**Source claim:** Integral Theory uses AQAL as shorthand for all quadrants, levels, lines,
states, and types.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent consciousness-related information along multiple
independent descriptive axes rather than reducing the architecture to a single scalar.

**Epistemic class:** philosophical/meta-theoretical framework.

### SC-WILBER-002
**Source claim:** The four quadrants distinguish individual interior, individual exterior,
collective interior, and collective exterior perspectives.

**Engineering extraction:** Add an explicit `perspective` coordinate to the state model.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-003
**Source claim:** The individual-interior perspective addresses subjective experience,
meaning, and first-person interpretation.

**Engineering extraction:** Keep internal/self-model state distinct from third-person
telemetry.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-004
**Source claim:** The individual-exterior perspective addresses observable behavior and
embodied or externally measurable structure.

**Engineering extraction:** Preserve body/tool/environment telemetry independently of the
agent's internal interpretation.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-005
**Source claim:** The collective-interior perspective concerns shared meaning, culture,
and intersubjective interpretation.

**Engineering extraction:** Represent socially negotiated meaning separately from private
self-state.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-006
**Source claim:** The collective-exterior perspective concerns systems, environments, and
observable collective organization.

**Engineering extraction:** Preserve environmental/system state as a layer distinct from
individual state.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-007
**Source claim:** Integral Theory distinguishes relatively enduring developmental levels
from changing states of consciousness.

**Engineering extraction:** Preserve the repository's distinction among `identity`,
`state`, and `regime`, rather than allowing a transient state to redefine identity.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-008
**Source claim:** Lines represent different capacities or intelligences that can develop
through levels.

**Engineering extraction:** Do not compress development or capability into one
consciousness scalar; preserve partially independent trajectories.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-009
**Source claim:** Types describe recurring styles that can cut across levels and states.

**Engineering extraction:** Model persistent operating-style variation without redefining
identity.

**Epistemic class:** framework construct.

### SC-WILBER-010
**Source claim:** Integral Theory is a comparative mapping framework rather than an
experimental proof of any particular consciousness mechanism.

**Engineering extraction:** Use AQAL primarily as a coverage and perspective check; require
separate causal evidence for mechanisms.

**Epistemic class:** epistemic boundary.
