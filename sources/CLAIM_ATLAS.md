## GNWT — Global Neuronal Workspace Theory

**Author / family:** Dehaene, Changeux et al.  
**Source class:** SCIENTIFIC/THEORY  
**Canonical source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.026

### SC-GNWT-001
**Source claim:** Conscious access is modeled as information becoming globally available to otherwise specialized processors.  
**Engineering extraction:** Implement a present workspace with controlled broadcast/read access.

### SC-GNWT-002
**Source claim:** The theory proposes a nonlinear amplification or ignition when a representation enters the global workspace.  
**Engineering extraction:** Test threshold-like activation and persistent amplification.

### SC-GNWT-003
**Source claim:** Recurrent processing contributes to sustaining globally available representations.  
**Engineering extraction:** Separate one-pass processing from recurrent workspace persistence.

### SC-GNWT-004
**Source claim:** Conscious contents can influence multiple downstream cognitive systems.  
**Engineering extraction:** Measure cross-module causal effects of one workspace state.

### SC-GNWT-005
**Source claim:** Local processing can occur without global conscious access.  
**Engineering extraction:** Use local-only baselines to avoid equating processing with consciousness.

### SC-GNWT-006
**Source claim:** Attention, working memory, and conscious access are related but not identical constructs.  
**Engineering extraction:** Keep attention, workspace, and memory as separate runtime variables.

### SC-GNWT-007
**Source claim:** Masking and threshold effects can be explained partly by whether information reaches global ignition.  
**Engineering extraction:** Use near-threshold perturbations as causal probes.

### SC-GNWT-008
**Source claim:** Long-range connectivity is important to the proposed broadcast architecture.  
**Engineering extraction:** Track cross-module communication rather than only local activation.

### SC-GNWT-009
**Source claim:** The theory distinguishes conscious access from the mere presence of sensory activity.  
**Engineering extraction:** Do not treat raw input activation as an integrated present.

### SC-GNWT-010
**Source claim:** GNWT is a testable theory with predictions about conscious versus nonconscious processing rather than a proof of machine consciousness.  
**Engineering extraction:** Tag GNWT-derived mechanisms as hypotheses and retain adversarial baselines.
