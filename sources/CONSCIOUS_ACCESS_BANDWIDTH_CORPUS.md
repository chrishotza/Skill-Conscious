# Conscious Access / Bandwidth — Source Matrix

This corpus informs the Conscious Interface / Bandwidth layer. It supports engineering hypotheses about bounded access, selective attention, report-independent analysis, and multidimensional temporal organization.

It does **not** establish that a machine with such mechanisms is phenomenally conscious.

## Tier A — No-report and covert-measure methodology

### Kronemer, Bandettini & Gonzalez-Castillo (2025)

Title: *Sleuthing subjectivity: a review of covert measures of consciousness*
Journal: Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 26, 476–496.
DOI: 10.1038/s41583-025-00934-1

Relevant point: overt report can be absent, unreliable, or methodologically confounding. No-report paradigms therefore matter when investigating consciousness.

Engineering extraction: access and present formation must remain operational when language/report is disabled.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-025-00934-1

## Tier A — Report-independent perceptual dynamics

### He et al. (2026)

Title: *Report-modulated prefrontal activity and consistent posterior representations during conscious visual perception*
Journal: Consciousness and Cognition, 142, 104075.
DOI: 10.1016/j.concog.2026.104075

Relevant point: the study reports neural representations of visible stimuli that persisted across report and no-report conditions, while some anterior activity increased specifically when report was required.

Engineering extraction: separate the operational present/access path from the language/report path.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2026.104075

## Tier A — Attention, memory and conscious processing

### D’Angiò et al. (2025)

Title: *The interplay of visual short-term memory, attention, and consciousness: A PRISMA systematic review of behavioral and neuroimaging studies with partial report and change detection*
Journal: Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 179, 106445.
DOI: 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106445

Relevant point: the review synthesizes evidence linking selective attention, visual short-term memory and multiple facets/stages of conscious processing.

Engineering extraction: model present formation as a selective interface rather than exposing all persistent state equally.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106445

## Tier A — Decision/report contamination

### Sánchez-Fuenzalida et al. (2026)

Title: *The act of detecting a stimulus contaminates measures of conscious experience with decision biases*
Journal: Nature Communications, 17, 6254.

Relevant point: detection judgments can introduce non-perceptual decision effects into measurements intended to characterize conscious experience.

Engineering extraction: do not treat an action/report decision as a transparent readout of internal experience.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72567-6

## Tier A — Temporal/dynamic framing

### Tagliazucchi (2020)

Title: *Time is a river which sweeps consciousness along, but consciousness is the river*
Journal: Physics of Life Reviews, 33, 75–77.
DOI: 10.1016/j.plrev.2019.09.010

Relevant point: consciousness is discussed through temporo-spatial dynamics rather than as a static label.

Engineering extraction: access state is a trajectory through changing runtime states, not a permanent boolean.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2019.09.010

## Tier A — Theoretical caution

### Cogitate Consortium et al. (2025)

Title: *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*
Journal: Nature, 642, 133–142.
DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1

Relevant point: major theories of consciousness remain actively contested.

Engineering extraction: do not hard-code one theory as if it were settled empirical fact. Use the repository's mechanisms as separable experimental constructs.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1

## Corpus conclusion

The most defensible engineering convergence is:

- report is not identical to consciousness;
- attention/access can be dissociated into distinct mechanisms;
- internal state availability can be selective and capacity-limited;
- conscious-processing experiments benefit from separating present/perceptual dynamics from report and decision;
- temporal state transitions matter.

Therefore Skill-Conscious should implement **bounded, persistent, runtime-owned access to the current present** and test whether manipulating that access changes downstream trajectories.

That is an architectural hypothesis, not a claim that bandwidth itself is consciousness.
