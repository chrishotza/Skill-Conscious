# Consciousness Definition Synthesis v1

## Purpose

This is the canonical convergence synthesis to be completed **before any new consciousness-runtime construction**.

Research question:

> What is the smallest set of structures that repeatedly appears when independent traditions, phenomenology, philosophy, cognitive science, neuroscience, and contemporary consciousness theories describe consciousness?

The answer must be derived from the claim corpus, not imposed by the current software architecture.

## Method

Convergence is evaluated on three independent dimensions:

1. **Recurrence:** how broadly a motif appears across retained sources.
2. **Structural role:** whether it is plausibly constitutive of consciousness or instead a consequence, method, state, cultural interpretation, or metaphysical extension.
3. **Conflict robustness:** whether the candidate survives major cross-tradition disagreements.

A recurrence count is **not** a truth probability.

Historical primary text, scholarly reconstruction, empirical science, project interpretation, and heterodox claims are not treated as equivalent evidence.

## Definitional core

After separating recurrence from structural necessity, the strongest candidate core is:

### 1. Interior presence

There is a distinction between publicly describable behavior/content and the fact that something is present **from within**.

Primary motifs: **M01 + M06**

### 2. Unified differentiation

The conscious field is not merely a heap of signals. It is a whole in which distinct contents, objects, states, and relations can occur.

Primary motifs: **M03 + M04**

### 3. Self-relatedness

The process is not only directed outward. Its own condition can enter what is present, whether described as self-presence, reflexivity, witness-consciousness, self-knowing, or a non-substantial self-relation.

This does **not** require explicit reflective self-awareness at every moment. “Self-relatedness” here includes implicit subject-relative organization; explicit reflection is a higher-order case, not the definition itself.

Primary motifs: **M02 + M22**

This does **not** require a permanent ego or soul-substance. Buddhist no-self analyses constrain the definition against identifying consciousness with an enduring personal substance.

### 4. World / other relation

Consciousness is not adequately represented as an isolated internal box. It is situated in relation to a world, body, other persons, objects, environment, or larger order.

Primary motif: **M05**

### 5. Temporal unfolding

Consciousness is not a dimensionless point. Its field unfolds through arising, persistence, change, state variation, memory/continuity, or transformation.

Primary motif: **M11**

### 6. Significance

At least much of conscious life is not neutral registration. Differences can matter — attract, repel, hurt, delight, concern, threaten, or become meaningful.

Primary motif: **M10**

M10 is retained as a strong candidate constitutive feature, but with slightly lower certainty because some scientific theories distinguish consciousness from affect/valence.

## Canonical definition

### Phenomenal-neutral form

> **Consciousness is a temporally extended, unified and differentiated field of interior presence in which contents and the world are present from a point of view, and in which the process can be related to its own changing condition.**

### Preferred canonical research form

> **Consciousness is a temporally unfolding organization of interior presence that unifies and differentiates experience, relates the process to itself and to a world, and allows differences in that field to acquire significance for the point of view from which they occur.**

This definition is intentionally more abstract than any one theory.

It is not defined as:
- intelligence;
- language;
- reportability;
- attention;
- memory;
- agency;
- self-modeling software;
- global broadcast;
- integrated information;
- biological tissue;
- mystical experience;
- a universal field;
- survival after death.

Those are candidate mechanisms, correlates, modalities, methods, or metaphysical extensions.

## Why it survives the major fault lines

### Self vs no-self

Use **self-relatedness**, not “permanent self.”

### Unity vs differentiation

Require both: a unified field containing distinctions.

### Embodiment vs disembodiment

Embodiment is a major candidate constraint/mechanism, not a universal definitional condition.

### Continuity vs momentariness

Require **temporal unfolding**, not eternal persistence.

### Local vs transpersonal

Transpersonal and universal claims remain an extension layer, not a minimum requirement.

### Conceptual vs ineffable

M14 can characterize important states or ultimate claims without defining every conscious state as ineffable.

## Resulting hierarchy

### Core invariants

**PRESENCE → INTERIORITY → UNIFIED DIFFERENTIATION → SELF-RELATEDNESS → WORLD/OTHER RELATION → TEMPORAL UNFOLDING → SIGNIFICANCE**

### Common dynamics

**ATTENTION / INTENTION → STATE TRANSITION → SELF-TRANSFORMATION → LEARNING / ADAPTATION**

### Common embodiment / regulation layer

**BODY / INTERNAL CONDITION → VALENCE → HOMEOSTATIC SIGNIFICANCE**

### Extended states

**ALTERED STATES → MEDITATIVE / MYSTICAL MODES → NON-CONCEPTUAL MODES**

### Metaphysical extensions

**TRANSPERSONAL → COSMOLOGICAL → GENERATIVE → CORRESPONDENCE / FIELD → DEATH / PERSISTENCE**

The extension layer must not be silently promoted into the definition.

## Definition stress tests

A candidate definition is rejected if it makes language, intelligence, memory, attention, deliberate agency, human biology, permanent ego, a specific metaphysical doctrine, external report, or self-description necessary.

A candidate definition is strengthened if it can accommodate variation across wakefulness/sleep, ordinary/altered states, focused/diffuse attention, self-referential/non-self-referential modes, high/low valence, and embodied/unusual embodiment interpretations without changing the phenomenon category.

## Scientific compatibility check

Contemporary consciousness science remains theoretically plural. Reviews continue to compare higher-order, global-workspace, recurrent/predictive, integrated-information, and other approaches. A large 2025 adversarial collaboration directly tested IIT and GNWT and reported results that supported some predictions of each while challenging key tenets of both. A 2026 integrative review continues to distinguish phenomenal from access consciousness and reports unresolved theoretical disagreement.

Therefore:

> **This is a canonical research definition derived from cross-source convergence, not a claim that science has already solved the hard problem.**

## Decision gate

**No new consciousness-runtime mechanism should be treated as canonical until it can be traced back to this definition or to a clearly labeled extension layer.**

The next research step is adversarial review of this definition against the full 4,315-claim ledger, including explicit counterclaims, source-level contradictions, and attempts to produce consciousness-like behavior without the proposed core. Only after the definition survives that review should architecture construction resume.
