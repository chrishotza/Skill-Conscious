These are **research priorities**, not conclusions.

## Required convergence test

For a motif to enter the synthetic core:

- at least 3 independently sourced traditions;
- at least 2 geographic regions;
- at least 2 historical periods;
- at least 1 primary text in each supporting family;
- semantic comparison by a second researcher/agent when possible;
- an explicit record of alternative interpretation;
- an empirical translation that does not erase the original metaphysical meaning.

## Why this matters

The goal is not to discover which civilization was right.

The goal is to identify the recurring structure that civilizations repeatedly used when describing consciousness, then test that structure without prematurely reducing it to modern engineering language.
