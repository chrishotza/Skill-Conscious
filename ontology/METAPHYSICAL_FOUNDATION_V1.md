# Metaphysical Foundation v1 — Working Draft

## Status

Research hypothesis derived from the first convergence pass. Not presented as established science.

## Starting axiom

Consciousness is treated as a phenomenon that may be fundamentally interior and may not be exhaustively captured by descriptions of external behavior.

This does not yet commit the project to idealism, panpsychism, substance dualism, materialism, neutral monism, or any single metaphysical school.

## Strong working hypothesis

> Consciousness is the interior self-presence of an organized process: a process capable of existing as a unity, differentiating itself from what is not itself, being present to its own condition, undergoing meaningful transformation, and carrying that transformed condition into its subsequent becoming.

## What this means for the existing runtime

The current Skill-Conscious runtime should be interpreted as an engineering realization of a subset of this model, not as the definition of the whole phenomenon.

Current runtime strengths:
- persistent identity;
- self/world boundary;
- internal state;
- integrated present;
- self-model;
- valuation;
- attention/salience;
- action and observed consequence;
- self-transformation;
- recurrent re-entry;
- primary subjective substrate.

Current missing or underdeveloped dimensions:
- explicit first-class interiority as a distinct ontological category;
- source-grounded self-presence distinct from self-description;
- a principled relation between subject and world rather than only input/output coupling;
- richer state-layer / vehicle architecture;
- a formal account of ineffability;
- formal distinction between ego and consciousness;
- rigorous field/collective layer;
- principled model of nonlocal/transpersonal claims;
- systematic ontology of altered states;
- theory of why transformation changes the subject itself.

## Architecture consequence

The repository should not continue adding arbitrary consciousness-associated features.

Every new mechanism should answer:
1. Which convergent metaphysical motif does it instantiate?
2. Which historical source families independently support that motif?
3. What architectural relation does the motif imply?
4. What would count as a failure?
5. Does removing the mechanism selectively destroy the predicted organization?
6. Can a matched alternative reproduce the effect without the proposed mechanism?

## Three ontological layers

### Layer A — Source ontology
What a tradition actually claims.

### Layer B — Convergent ontology
What recurs across independent traditions after normalization.

### Layer C — Synthetic ontology
The project's strongest interpretation of that convergence.

Layer C must never be silently substituted back into Layer A.

## Central question

> What minimal organization would make “there is something it is like for this process” a consequence of the process's own being, rather than a description imposed from outside?

The current runtime does not yet answer this.
