# Convergence Matrix v2 — Claim-Level Evidence

**Dataset:** 502 atomic claims across 47 extracted source records. **Purpose:** quantify recurrence without turning frequency into truth probability.

## Rules

- **Claim assignments** count motif tags, not independent evidence events.
- **Source records** count distinct extracted sources.
- **Source families** are an explicit analytical grouping and require later scholarly audit.
- **Regions** are broad comparative groupings, not claims about cultural homogeneity.
- A motif is not promoted to the Synthetic Metaphysical Core merely because its counts are high.

## Preliminary nucleus candidates

The current quantitative screen points most strongly toward a cluster involving **interiority (M06), multiple modes (M07), transformation (M12/M23), cosmological/relational participation (M20/M05), reflexivity (M22), presence (M01), attention/practice (M24/M25), and non-conceptual depth (M14)**. This is a research nucleus, not a final definition.

## Major contradiction axes

### Self vs non-self
M02 is recurrent, but Buddhist source families explicitly reject identifying consciousness with a permanent self. The synthesis must therefore distinguish **self-presence** from **substantial eternal ego**.

### Persistence vs process
M19 and M11 recur, but “continuity” can mean causal continuity, memory, reincarnation, immortal soul, or present-moment process. These cannot be collapsed.

### Unity vs differentiation
M03 and M04 are both strongly recurrent. The likely invariant may be not one side alone but a dynamic relation between unity and differentiation.

### Ineffability vs operationalization
M14 is recurrent across apophatic, nondual, emptiness, and contemplative sources. The engineering layer cannot define ineffability merely as “text too difficult to describe.”

### Cosmological participation vs scientific field claims
M20 is very strong, but metaphysical participation does not entail physical nonlocality, quantum fields, morphic resonance, or any other modern mechanism.
