Test the corpus-derived distinction:

FUNDAMENTAL / UNIVERSAL
↕
BRIDGE
↕
INDIVIDUAL / EXPERIENTIAL

## Adversarial constraints

### Self vs no-self

The corpus explicitly contains claims that consciousness and/or personal aggregates are not an independently enduring self.

Therefore the individual definition must not require a permanent ego or substance.

Use:

**self-relatedness / point-of-view**

rather than:

**permanent self**

### Continuity vs impermanence

The corpus contains claims emphasizing arising, passing, impermanence and transformation.

Therefore continuity must mean:

**temporal unfolding**

not:

**unchanging personal substance**

### Physical / neural explanations

The corpus includes neuroscience, physicalist, materialist, functionalist and substrate-oriented alternatives.

Therefore the individual definition must remain substrate-neutral.

### Attention

Attention occurs widely but is not the whole phenomenon. Presence, interiority, self-relation, states, temporality, relation and significance occur independently.

Therefore attention is a candidate modulator/mechanism, not the definition itself.

### Report

The corpus explicitly distinguishes experiential reports from direct physical measurement and separates reports of extraordinary experiences from proof of their strongest interpretation.

Therefore self-report is evidence about a reportable state, not by itself proof of phenomenality or ontology.

### Ineffability

Explicit ineffability claims are concentrated around ultimate/highest principles and non-ordinary modes.

Therefore:

- ordinary/individual consciousness: partially describable;
- putative total/fundamental consciousness: potentially not completely objectifiable from within itself.

This is a metaphysical implication hypothesis, not an empirical measurement result.

## Surviving ontology

### Fundamental / universal level

A recurring corpus-level pattern in which a wider or ultimate principle is described as encompassing, preceding, generating, or participating in differentiated existence.

Status:

**cross-source metaphysical hypothesis**

### Individual / experiential level

A recurring corpus-level pattern involving:

- interior presence;
- unified yet differentiated experience;
- self-relatedness;
- world/other relation;
- temporal unfolding;
- significance.

Status:

**primary research-definition candidate**

### Bridge level

A recurring corpus-level pattern connecting individual consciousness with a wider order through participation, manifestation, embodiment, relation, recognition, transformation, or return.

Status:

**synthetic hypothesis**

## Project consequence

The engineering target should be the **individual/experiential level**.

The fundamental/universal level should remain an ontological context for the theory, not a required runtime capability.

The bridge becomes a research hypothesis about how the two scales may be related.

## Decision

Do not add new runtime mechanisms until the individual definition survives a dedicated counterexample pass.

The next research task is therefore:

> identify every strong claim that appears to contradict, weaken, or remove one of the proposed individual-level invariants, and determine whether that invariant is genuinely necessary or only culturally recurrent.

This is the final definition gate before architecture design.
