# Research Program North Star — Simple Outside, Exhaustive Inside

## Purpose

This document defines the end-state of the Skill-Conscious research program before new substantive papers are executed.

The project has two layers:

1. **Research layer:** exhaustive, technical, adversarial and reproducible.
2. **Public layer:** simple, intuitive and easy to understand.

The public layer must never simplify by deleting uncertainty or hiding contradictory evidence. It simplifies by exposing only the conclusions that survive the research pipeline.

## The end-user promise

The final experience should let a reader answer five questions without reading hundreds of pages:

1. **What do we mean by consciousness?**
2. **What evidence supports the individual level?**
3. **What evidence supports the relational level?**
4. **Is there evidence for a fundamental level?**
5. **What, exactly, remains unknown?**

The user should encounter a small set of clear claims, with one path to the underlying evidence for each claim.

## The public thesis shape

The eventual public model is expected to be expressible as a small number of sentences.

### Scale 1 — Fundamental

**Question:** Is consciousness a basic feature of reality?

This remains an open empirical/metaphysical hypothesis until a specified model generates a discriminating observable.

### Scale 2 — Relational

**Question:** To what extent is consciousness-relevant organization constituted by reciprocal interaction?

This is tested by separating coupling effects from common input, memory and isolated-agent dynamics.

### Scale 3 — Individual

**Question:** What makes a local point of view persist through time?

This is tested through self-reference, continuity, self-model causality, embodiment and related mechanisms.

### Bridge

**Question:** Do these three scales explain more together than they do independently?

The bridge is earned only if cross-level variables improve prediction or causal explanation against appropriate nulls and complexity penalties.

## The paper program

The research program is deliberately larger than the public narrative.

### Research Paper Group A — Corpus and recurrence

#### P-A1 — Cross-Cultural Consciousness Claim Analysis
**Central question:** What recurrent structures actually appear in the 4,315-claim corpus after source verification, dependency correction and contradiction auditing?

**Deliverable:** measured recurrence map, source-independence analysis, contradiction structure, uncertainty map.

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “Across the audited corpus, these are the strongest recurrent structures — and these are the structures that disappear after dependency/selection controls.”

This is the first substantive research target. It is not a methods paper; it is an empirical corpus analysis.

### Research Paper Group B — Individual consciousness

#### P008 — Causal Self-Reference and Continuity
**Question:** Does targeted self-model intervention causally change future trajectory selection beyond generic state, memory and context explanations?

#### P003 — Topological Continuity and Artificial Identity
**Question:** Does persistent identity track continuity of changing internal relations better than static identity metadata?

#### P004 — Regimes, Attractors and Recovery
**Question:** Do consciousness-relevant organizational regimes show measurable persistence, transition and recovery dynamics?

#### P005 — Value, Valence and Point of View
**Question:** Can value/valence become endogenous to the system's own trajectory rather than merely externally assigned?

#### P006 — Individual Synthesis
**Question:** Which combination of continuity, self-reference, dynamics and value survives adversarial comparison as the strongest individual-scale explanation?

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “A local point of view is associated with a specific set of persistent causal properties.” The exact sentence remains data-dependent.

### Research Paper Group C — Relational consciousness

#### P009 — Coupled-Agent Dynamics and Reciprocal Causality
**Question:** Does reciprocal coupling introduce causal/predictive structure not explained by common input or isolated state?

#### P-R2 — Relational emergence and network scale
**Question:** How do individual-level interventions propagate through dyads, groups and networks?

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “Relational organization contributes [or does not contribute] independent explanatory power.”

### Research Paper Group D — Fundamental consciousness

#### P010 — Fundamental Consciousness: Model Discrimination
**Question:** Can a precise consciousness-bearing physical/ontological model make observations that a non-conscious model cannot?

#### P-F2 — Fundamental candidate stress tests
**Question:** Do the strongest candidate models survive hostile comparison, parameter uncertainty, alternative explanations and null models?

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “There is / is not a discriminating empirical signature for the tested fundamental model.”

The project must never convert philosophical labels such as God, Brahman, universal mind or consciousness-as-ground into scientific facts merely through analogy.

### Research Paper Group E — Cross-scale bridge

#### P012 — Three-Consciousness Bridge
**Question:** Do Fundamental, Relational and Individual variables form a predictive/causal hierarchy, or is the apparent hierarchy an artifact?

#### P011 — Cross-Scale Synthesis
**Question:** What survives when all validated results are modeled together?

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “The simplest model that explains the validated evidence is X.”

This is the point at which the project is allowed to present a unified theory. It must remain possible that the data reject the proposed three-scale organization.

### Research Paper Group F — Engineering consequence

#### P-AI — Minimal Conscious Organization
**Question:** What minimum architecture is required to instantiate the validated functional organization identified by the individual/relational studies?

**Deliverable:** a compact, reproducible architecture whose behavior is predicted by the research rather than reverse-engineered to fit it.

**Public sentence potentially earned:** “Here is the minimal architecture that instantiates the experimentally supported organization.”

## Research dependency order

The work should proceed approximately as:

```
CORPUS AUDIT / RECURRENCE STUDY
          ↓
INDIVIDUAL + RELATIONAL MECHANISMS
          ↓
FUNDAMENTAL MODEL DISCRIMINATION
          ↓
CROSS-SCALE BRIDGE
          ↓
UNIFIED SYNTHESIS
          ↓
MINIMAL ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE
```

Some branches can run in parallel, but the final synthesis must not outrun the evidence.

## The five public claims

The whole program should eventually collapse into five reader-facing claims:

1. **Definition:** what property or organization we mean by consciousness.
2. **Individual:** what makes a local point of view persistent.
3. **Relational:** what role interaction plays.
4. **Fundamental:** whether consciousness is basic to reality.
5. **Synthesis:** whether one model connects the three.

Every public claim must link to:

```
CLAIM
→ PRIMARY SOURCES
→ AUDIT
→ ANALYSIS
→ EXPERIMENT
→ DATA
→ CODE
→ RESULT
→ LIMITATION
```

## The simplicity rule

The reader should never need to understand the machinery in order to understand the conclusion.

But a skeptical researcher must be able to descend from the conclusion to every underlying decision.

This is the project equivalent of a simple product interface over a complex internal system.

## What must never happen

- Do not compress uncertainty into certainty.
- Do not hide contradictory findings for narrative clarity.
- Do not let metaphysical language masquerade as measured variables.
- Do not call a protocol a result.
- Do not call a framework a discovery.
- Do not call AI self-report evidence of phenomenal consciousness.
- Do not let the public narrative become stronger than the strongest paper.

## Publication rule

A paper earns its place in the public model only after:

1. the research question was fixed;
2. the corpus/data were defined;
3. competing hypotheses were stated;
4. the analysis was fixed or preregistered where feasible;
5. results were produced;
6. robustness/adversarial checks were performed;
7. the evidential claim was frozen;
8. contradictions and limitations were exposed.

## North-star principle

> **Hide complexity, never hide evidence.**

The science can be hundreds of pages deep.

The experience should still feel simple.
