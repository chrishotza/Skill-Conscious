# Research Method North Star

## Status
**Canonical methodological direction — established 2026-10-06**

This document defines the methodological north star for the entire Skill-Conscious research program.

## Core principle
> **Be radical in the hypothesis space; be conservative in the inferential step.**

The project is allowed to investigate fundamental consciousness, relational consciousness, non-standard ontologies, panpsychist and idealist proposals, anomalous phenomena, contemplative traditions, mystical and esoteric claims, machine consciousness and other heterodox hypotheses.

No hypothesis receives evidential privilege because it is conventional or unconventional.

The standard is the same:

~~~
CLAIM
  ↓
SOURCE VERIFICATION
  ↓
PROVENANCE
  ↓
INDEPENDENCE / DEPENDENCE
  ↓
CONSTRUCT
  ↓
COMPETING HYPOTHESES
  ↓
OPERATIONALIZATION
  ↓
FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION
  ↓
PRE-REGISTRATION / ANALYSIS FREEZE
  ↓
EXPERIMENT / OBSERVATION
  ↓
RESULT
  ↓
REPLICATION
  ↓
INFERENCE
~~~

## What the corpus is for

The closed corpus currently contains:
- 325 registered source records;
- 328 effective source IDs;
- 4,315 effective claim records;
- 4,315 unique global claim IDs.

These counts describe corpus coverage. They do not imply that every claim has already received passage-level verification, independent corroboration, or empirical support.

The corpus is therefore a **hypothesis-generation and evidence-traceability substrate**, not a truth database.

## Paper architecture

The program has five methodological layers:
1. Corpus paper — how claims are collected, verified, linked, adjudicated and converted into research objects.
2. Framework papers — definitions and competing explanatory models.
3. Mechanism papers — operational hypotheses at the Individual and Relational scales.
4. Fundamental-model papers — explicit physical/ontological models with discriminating observables.
5. Synthesis papers — only after lower-level results exist.

No synthesis paper may retroactively turn an unresolved hypothesis into a fact.

## Claim admissibility

A claim can be cited as a central premise only when:
1. source identity is recoverable;
2. source wording or a precise paraphrase is recoverable;
3. translation status is explicit where relevant;
4. project interpretation is separated from source statement;
5. provenance is recorded;
6. contradictions are retained;
7. the intended construct is defined;
8. at least one competing interpretation is available.

A claim failing these tests may remain useful as historical, contextual or exploratory material, but must not carry the central causal burden of a paper.

## Evidence maturity ladder

This ladder describes **research maturity**, not truth.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ES0 | Catalogued claim; source registered |
| ES1 | Passage/source record verified |
| ES2 | Source-level corroboration and contradiction audit completed |
| ES3 | Construct and operational definition fixed |
| ES4 | Prediction preregistered/frozen and test executed |
| ES5 | Independent replication or strong external confirmation |

The paper must report the highest ES actually achieved for each central claim.

## Source independence

Frequency is not independence.

The project must distinguish:
- independent observation;
- independent author;
- independent textual lineage;
- independent translation;
- independent dataset;
- derivative quotation;
- later commentary;
- common cultural transmission;
- unknown dependency.

Repeated claims from dependent sources must not be counted as independent confirmations.

Where practical, the repository should represent these relations as a source-dependency graph.

## Contradiction policy

For every major claim family, search explicitly for:
- supporting sources;
- contradictory sources;
- alternative interpretations;
- boundary cases;
- negative cases;
- null results.

A hypothesis is not weakened by discovering a credible contradiction. The contradiction is part of the evidence.

## Operationalization rule

Words such as consciousness, energy, vibration, field, unity, soul, spirit and information are not experimental variables merely because they appear in a source.

They must be translated into a measurable construct, or the statement remains philosophical/interpretive.

The translation itself must be labeled as a project interpretation.

## Adversarial rule

Every consequential paper must include:
- a primary hypothesis;
- a null;
- at least one serious competing hypothesis;
- a failure criterion;
- analysis decisions fixed before outcome inspection whenever feasible.

The project should prefer designs in which a surprising result would change the theory rather than merely generate another explanatory paragraph.

## Preregistration and registration

For confirmatory experiments, the project should prefer preregistration or a Registered Report-style protocol.

Pre-registration should fix, when applicable:
- hypotheses;
- primary/secondary outcomes;
- sample or run plan;
- exclusion criteria;
- analysis pipeline;
- statistical/model comparison plan;
- stopping rules;
- interpretation boundaries.

Deviations must be reported rather than silently replacing the original plan.

## Scientific status vocabulary

Every repository artifact must label statements as one of:
- SOURCE CLAIM
- PROJECT HYPOTHESIS
- OPERATIONAL DEFINITION
- EMPIRICAL PREDICTION
- PROTOCOL
- EMPIRICAL RESULT
- INFERENCE
- OPEN QUESTION

These statuses are not interchangeable.

## Publication gate

A paper is not complete because the prose is complete.

Before preprint/release, central claims must have:
- traceable claim IDs;
- source and provenance audit;
- explicit competing explanations;
- operational definitions;
- frozen or preregistered predictions where feasible;
- controls/nulls;
- executable analysis;
- data/code provenance;
- negative-result handling;
- interpretation proportional to evidence.

## Radicality rule

Going beyond conventional science means expanding the set of models that can be tested.

It does **not** mean lowering source criticism, measurement standards, reproducibility, falsifiability, statistical discipline, or the distinction between observation and inference.

> **Expand the hypothesis space. Do not weaken the evidence standard.**

## Current first-paper target

**P000 — A Machine-Readable Cross-Cultural Corpus of Consciousness Claims: Provenance, Phenomenology, Ontology and Testability**

P000 is the methodological foundation for the rest of the paper program. It should establish the corpus as an auditable research object and define the transformation from source material to empirical hypothesis.

## Reference methodology anchors

- PRISMA 2020 — reporting framework for systematic reviews and evidence synthesis.
- PRISMA-ScR — reporting framework for scoping reviews.
- OSF registrations/preregistration — time-stamped, read-only records of research plans.
- Registered Reports — peer review of hypotheses/methods before outcome is known.

These are methodological anchors, not rigid templates. The repository should adapt them to a cross-cultural consciousness corpus and declare every deviation.
